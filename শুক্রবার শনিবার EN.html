English Paragraph Writing (মডেল প্যারাগ্রাফ)
৫টি প্যারাগ্রাফ
Model Paragraph

Physical Exercise: Physical exercise means the regular movement of our limbs according to rules. It is essential for maintaining sound health and physical fitness. It strengthens our muscles, improves blood circulation, and prevents various chronic diseases. Walking, swimming, jogging, and playing outdoor sports are common forms of exercise. A sound mind resides in a sound body, and regular exercise keeps both body and mind fresh, energetic, and active. Without proper physical exercise, one cannot maintain vitality, health, and a cheerful spirit in life.

Model Paragraph

Dhaka Metro Rail: Metro rail is a modern and important addition to the transportation system of Bangladesh. It has been introduced to reduce traffic congestion and provide safe, fast, and comfortable travel, especially in Dhaka city. The metro rail service began its operation in 2022 and has already brought significant changes to daily commuting. It runs on elevated tracks and uses electric trains, which are environment-friendly and energy efficient.

The metro rail helps save valuable time by avoiding traffic jams that are common on city roads. Thousands of passengers can travel at a time, which reduces the pressure on buses and private vehicles. As a result, road accidents and air pollution are also decreasing. The stations are well designed, clean, and equipped with modern facilities, ensuring convenience for passengers of all ages. Metro rail also plays an important role in economic development. It makes communication faster, helps people reach workplaces on time, and increases productivity. Moreover, it creates employment opportunities and encourages planned urban development. Although the project faced challenges such as high cost and construction difficulties, its benefits are undeniable. Metro rail has opened a new era of modern transportation in Bangladesh and is expected to expand further in the future.

Model Paragraph

Duty and Responsibility of a parents: Parents are the first and most important guides in a child's life. They have many duties and responsibilities towards their children. First of all, they provide food, clothing, shelter, education and proper healthcare. They also ensure a safe and peaceful environment for their children. Parents teach good manners, honesty, discipline, kindness and respect for others. They help their children distinguish between right and wrong and encourage them to develop good habits. Besides, parents should pay attention to their children's studies, mental health and character-building. They should listen to their problems and give proper advice with love and patience. Parents also protect their children from bad company and harmful activities. At the same time, they should encourage independence and responsibility. By setting a good example through their own behavior, parents can shape the future of their children. Therefore, responsible parents play a vital role in building good citizens and a better society.
